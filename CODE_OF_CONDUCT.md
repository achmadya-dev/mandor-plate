# Code of Conduct

Mandor Plate contributors are expected to keep project spaces professional, inclusive, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to madyardwn@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled proportionately. This policy applies to repository discussions, issue trackers, reviews, and project-related public interactions.
